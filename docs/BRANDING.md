# Launch Rail artwork

[English](BRANDING.md) · [Deutsch](BRANDING-de.md)

The existing Launch Rail identity is retained: dark petrol, cream, mint and an orange shortcut arrow. The graphic shows a toolbar linking websites and tools. Slogan: Many sites. One move. / Viele Sites. Ein Handgriff.

Runtime artwork is local in assets/brand. GitHub presentation PNG/SVG banners are 1280 × 640 and kept separately in assets-github, in English and German. Those derivatives retain the established artwork; no new design variant is introduced. PNG icons are 128 × 128 and 256 × 256 with SVG source. WordPress update banners are 772 × 250 and 1544 × 500.
